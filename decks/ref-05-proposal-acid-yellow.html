About the studio

northbeam is a pricing and packaging studio. We are eleven people in two cities, and we have shipped forty-two revenue systems since 2039 — most of them for teams who had already tried twice on their own.

We work in one mode only: a fixed scope, a fixed window, a named owner on both sides. No retainers, no discovery phase that pays for itself. The first deliverable lands in week two, and it is a decision, not a document.

Every engagement ends with the client running it without us.

Handover is a deliverable, not a courtesy. Two weeks of pairing are written into the scope of every proposal we send.
