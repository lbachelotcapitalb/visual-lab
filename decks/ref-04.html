+travel Problem Series A — 2026

The
problem

Independent operators still sell offline, and lose a third of their margin to intermediaries they never chose.

01

Fragmented inventory. 68% of small operators still keep availability in a spreadsheet, and republish it by hand on four channels.

02

Commission drift. Marketplace fees moved from 12% to 21% in five years, without any matching gain in booking volume.

03

No direct relationship. The operator never owns the traveller record, so repeat business is captured by someone else.

+travel Solution Series A — 2026

The
solution

↗

One layer between the operator and every channel — no commission on direct bookings, ever.

We sit under the operator's own site and under the marketplaces they already sell on. Same inventory, same prices, one place to change them.

01

Connect. Import existing inventory in one pass, no migration project, no downtime for the operator.

02

Publish. Every channel reads the same calendar, so overbooking stops being a daily problem.

03

Own. The traveller record and the payment relationship stay on the operator's side.
